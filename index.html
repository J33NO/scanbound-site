Every barcode is a Hero.

Scan the codes around you, reveal the Hero hiding in each one, and build a team that can outlast every rival.

How it plays

  1. Scan

    Point your camera at any barcode or QR code: a cereal box, a book, a soda can. Every code holds a Hero.

  2. Reveal

    Meet your Main Hero. You don't choose it: which Hero it is, its weapon, rarity and stats all come from the code you scanned.

  3. Build

    Recruit two Supporting Heroes as the run goes, trade up in the shop, and drag your team into formation on the board.

  4. VS

    Battle

    Auto-chess battles: your team fights on its own, round after round, against rival Tacticians. Every run is a fresh roguelike climb. Be the last Tactician standing to win.

Every code is its own Hero

The same barcode always reveals the same Hero, on any phone. Swap codes with friends, hunt for the rare ones, and fill your HeroDex with dozens of Heroes and every look they can wear.

Every run you finish unlocks that Hero's full details in your HeroDex. The more Heroes you know, the better you can plan synergies and build stronger teams in future runs.

Fair by design

  • Plays offline. No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads. Nothing to watch, nothing in your way.
  • No tracking. Scanbound collects no data. No photos are ever taken.
  • Free to play. One optional Supporter Pack supports the developer and shows your appreciation. As a thank-you, it unlocks every variant of every Hero, ready to equip on each Hero you know. Cosmetics only: it never changes anything in battle.
