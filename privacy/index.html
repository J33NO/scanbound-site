Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

This policy covers the Scanbound game on iPhone, iPad and Android.

In short

Scanbound collects no data. The game works offline, has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and never sends anything about you to the developer or anyone else. Everything it keeps stays on your device.

The camera

Scanbound asks to use your camera so it can read barcodes and QR codes. The camera view is read live, on your device, only to find a code. No photos are taken, saved or sent.

You can say no, or turn camera access off at any time in your device's Settings.

What stays on your device

To play, Scanbound saves a few things in its own storage on your device:

  • Your game: your Heroes, your collection, your current run, and the values of the codes you have scanned, so the game remembers which Hero each code revealed.
  • Your settings: graphics, audio, tips and similar choices.
  • A small diagnostic history: a record of errors - what failed, the app and system version, and the error details - to help fix problems. It does not include barcode values, your saved game, or the names of your Heroes.

None of this is uploaded by Scanbound. Your device's own backup service (such as iCloud Backup or Android backup) may include it, under Apple's or Google's terms. Deleting Scanbound from your device deletes all of it.

Sharing a diagnostic report

If something goes wrong, Options has a Share Diagnostic Report button. It opens your device's share sheet, and you choose where the report goes, if anywhere. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

If you email a report or a question to the developer, it is used only to answer you and fix the problem, is never shared or sold, and is deleted when it is no longer needed.

Purchases

The optional Supporter Pack is bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment under their own privacy policies. The developer never receives your payment details. Scanbound only records on your device that the pack is owned, and checks with the store to restore it.

Children

Scanbound collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. If Scanbound ever starts collecting data, the policy will say so before it happens.
